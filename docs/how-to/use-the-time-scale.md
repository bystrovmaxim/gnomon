# Use the time scale

Every page in Maxitor can be read as it was at any past moment, and compared with another. One control does it for every page: the **pill** on the right of the top row.

## Read a past moment

1. Click the pill. A card opens under it with the page's history: bars of what changed — added green, changed amber, removed red — and, above them, points of what mattered, such as a publication.
2. Click a moment on the scale, or a point. The whole screen shows that moment: the page, the tree and anyone following you. The pill turns blue and names the moment; the address holds it as `at`, so a link or a reload gives the same.
3. To come back, click **Now** beside the pill.

The green dashed line is "now"; what lies right of it is hatched and cannot be chosen. In the past a page is read only: nothing can be written there, except what the page itself offers on a point — the template's **Roll back to v3**, which publishes v3's notes again as a new publication.

Move on the scale as on a map: drag across a stretch to zoom into it, use the wheel to zoom round the pointer, Shift-wheel to move; on a phone, drag to move and pinch to zoom. The presets 1 min, 10 min, 1 h, 1 d, 7 d, 30 d and All show a stretch at once. Hover a point to read its card; hover a bar to see what it counts. The "i" explains every mark the page uses. Shift + ← → step to the previous or the next change from anywhere.

## Compare two moments

The card's switch offers what the page can draw:

- **Diff** shows the later moment, B, with what changed since the earlier one, A: each changed element wears its colour and a soft halo, the rest is faded. The pill counts the changes, "+2 −1 ~1". A stepper in the page's corner walks them — Shift + ↑ ↓ from anywhere — and opens each one's card.
- **Side by side** shows A on the left and B on the right, each with its head. With **Track** on, what you point at in one panel lights up in the other, and what you choose there is brought into view. When the other panel cannot show it, it says why: "Not in A", or "Hidden by the filters". On a phone one panel shows at a time, with an A / B switch.

Move A or B by dragging its handle on the scale, or click near it. To return to one moment, choose **One**.
