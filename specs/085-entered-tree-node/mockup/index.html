A tree node that is entered

spec 085 · issue #122. A node either expands under itself or is entered: the left panel then holds only its children, and the node stands at the head of the panel.

Screens
Device

What to look at

  • A node that expands has the arrow; a node that is entered has › on its right and shows no children until it is clicked.
  • An entered level holds only the children of its node; the node itself stands at the head, across the width.
  • The head of the tree has two buttons: collapse and the arrow, which is Back — off at the root.
  • Back returns to the level before the last entering and keeps what was expanded there; it opens no page.
  • The crumbs name the organization, the entered nodes and the open page, never a node passed by expanding; a click on a crumb steps out to its level.
  • A reload rebuilds the levels from the address; what was expanded in them is not kept.
