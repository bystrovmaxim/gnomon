# Changelog

All notable changes to `maxitor` (until 2.3.0 published as `aoa-maxitor`) are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **The time scale.** One time control for every page: the pill on the right of the top row, with its arrows, the moment and Now, and the card hanging 11 px under it with the scale — a moment chosen with a click or a handle, written to the address as `at`, kept to the minute and never later than the watermark; "now" a green dashed line with the future hatched; presets, a drag across a stretch, the wheel and a pinch to zoom. The open page sets it up through its history, format 2: bars of its changes read in chunks and kept below the watermark, points of its own kinds on a lane, a card of the plugin's blocks, the legend from the setup, the comment of the milestone in force; what a page did not promise is left out and named in the console. Diff and Side by side add `mode` and `from` to the address: the core lays out the panels, gives each page its context, relays Track between them by anchors with its notes, passes Shift + ↑ ↓ on as `panel.step`, and shows the page's counts on the pill. The frame as the graph viewer draws it: the canvas's surface and dots, the tree's Material pictograms, the filters' disks, the account's circle in its colour, the crumbs of the sections entered, Back through the address and absent at the root, the collapsed tree a strip with one button. The test page's fixtures show bars alone, milestones alone, both, the graph's kinds with small points and flags and an undeclared kind; the browser check walks them. Maxitor 4.11.0, the core's application 1.10.0. ([#98](https://github.com/bystrovmaxim/gnomon/issues/98))

- **A page that loads fast is never taken for silent.** The panel listens for a page's messages from the moment it puts the page's frame in the document, so a plugin page that says it is ready before a busy screen finishes rendering is heard; before, it could be shown as "This page does not answer". ([#122](https://github.com/bystrovmaxim/gnomon/issues/122))

- **A node in the tree opens two ways.** A node says in `navigation.yaml` whether it expands — its children appear under it — or is entered: the left panel then holds only its children, with the node at the head across its width. The frame keeps the levels; the head's arrow is Back, which pops the last entering, keeps what was expanded in the level and opens no page, and the up it was is gone (FR-018 of bystrovmaxim/gnomon#109 amended); the top row's crumbs are built from the entered levels and the open page only, and a crumb steps the panel out to its level. An address names the page alone, so a reload or a link enters every entered node above it. Navigation format 4 adds `opens`, required on every node with children and refused on one without, each refusal naming the node; format 3 is read as it is, every node expanding; the column `opens` arrives with migration `plugins/0008_a_node_opens.sql`. The core's Settings and the template's Notes are entered. Maxitor 4.10.0, the core's application 1.9.0. ([#122](https://github.com/bystrovmaxim/gnomon/issues/122))

- **The template's draft over everyone's published notes.** The plugin template the core is built and checked with has a Draft of each person's own changes and a read-only Published page; its notes change only when a person publishes the whole draft as one revision, overwriting or giving up each conflict with someone else's publication. The core's browser check (`check-core.mjs`) walks the draft's privacy, its states and reverts, publishing with a release, Published's marks and Edit in the draft, and the phone; the builder's npm list adds `diff` for the template's marking of changed words. ([#121](https://github.com/bystrovmaxim/gnomon/issues/121))

- **The template's pages now.** Its notes lie on the canvas as cards or a table; "New note" opens a dialog, a card edits its note through `changed_since` and `deleted` in words, deleting asks once, and a colleague's note appears without a reload when "now" moves, marked new; Published notes shows the revision in force, Draft the changes since it and publishing. The core's browser check walks it on a desktop and a phone. Maxitor 4.8.0, the core's application 1.7.0. ([#102](https://github.com/bystrovmaxim/gnomon/issues/102))
- **A page gives the core its history; navigation.yaml holds the tree only.** `PanelPage` takes a page's history — its reading action, bars, milestones, card, comparisons and legend's words — and sends it as `panel.history` when the page opens and whenever it gives a new one; the core keeps the last for the open page and checks every name against the version's actions and its passport's comparisons, kept when the version is checked (migration `plugins/0007_page_gives_history.sql`; the organization's answer carries them as `histories`), refusing a name that does not hold by name and keeping the rest. Navigation format 3 is the tree only: format 2 and a node declaring `history`, `room` or `controls` are refused, the node named. A room shares no parameter of a page's address, and `surface` is gone: pointers travel on the canvas only. Maxitor 4.7.0, the core's application 1.6.0, `maxitor-web` 1.28.0, the installer 1.5.0 and the runner 1.4.0 for their graph files. ([#116](https://github.com/bystrovmaxim/gnomon/issues/116))
- **The shell's filters in full and lists lying whole on the canvas.** `@maxitor/panel` 0.4.0: a page's list is a card at full length on the canvas, the rows in view drawn and the head sticking; the filters come in three kinds — a choice with a search and "N more", a text, a range of numbers or dates; the date range picker and the date picker open like functions; a sheet from below on a phone. The core keeps a page's hidden anchors (`panel.hidden`); its test page shows a list of 10,000 accounts filtered by every kind at `/w/<code>/core/panel-test/list`; the browser check walks it on a desktop and a phone. Maxitor 4.6.0, the core's application 1.5.0, `@maxitor/tokens` 0.4.0. ([#115](https://github.com/bystrovmaxim/gnomon/issues/115))
- **The core builds every plugin's frontend with its own `@maxitor/panel`.** A plugin's wheel carries its frontend as sources — passport format 3 names their folder in `frontend.source`; format 2, which carried a built site, is refused by name. Before deploying a version the installer builds its frontend in the builder image of the running core, `maxitor-plugin-builder:<core version>`, made once on the host from the `maxitor` package: the lock must match `package.json`, every `@maxitor/*` package becomes the core's own copy, every other package must be on the core's npm list and come from npm, the packages are fetched without running any of the plugin's code and built by `next build` in a container with no network. A version whose build fails is not deployed: the install fails with the first error, such as `app/[org]/page.tsx(12,7): error TS2322: …`, and `FrontendBuildFailedEvent` names the plugin and its version. Builds are kept in `plugins.plugin_build`, one per version and core version. After an upgrade of the core every enabled version is built again in the background while the old builds keep serving; the site moves to the new builds, all at once, only when every one is built (`plugins.frontend_generation`, `FrontendGenerationMovedEvent`), and the runner restarts each frontend on its new image. An author runs the same step with `maxitor plugin build <path> --core <version>`; the local stand lets its installer build the template; CI builds the template with the checkout's core and with the newest release its `requires.maxitor` allows. Maxitor 4.5.0, `maxitor-web` 1.27.0, the installer 1.4.0, the runner 1.3.0. ([#110](https://github.com/bystrovmaxim/gnomon/issues/110))
- **The core's browser check measures the shell.** `check-core.mjs` refuses filters that are not 148 px wide or touch the page's content, and expects the zoom first in the bottom bar as the mockup orders it; Next.js's development badge is off, so it covers neither the tree's foot nor the bar. Maxitor 4.4.2 with the core's application 1.4.1.
- **Every page draws its controls in the shell of `@maxitor/panel`.** The core draws nothing over the right panel: `Controls` and its styles are gone, and so is a node's `controls` — the core reads navigation format 2 only and refuses format 1 by name, migration `plugins/0004_plugin_node_no_controls.sql` drops the column from `plugin_node` and its versions, and the organization's tree answers no `controls`. The frame refuses a page's change of `at`, `mode`, `from` or a `_…` name and a parameter named like a path parameter, and marks a page whose `panel.ready` says it has no shell with a red strip when `NEXT_PUBLIC_MAXITOR_MARK_UNFRAMED=1` (the local stand sets it) or in development. The tree's head has collapse and up. The panel's test page shows a drawing in the shell at `/w/<code>/core/panel-test/drawing` and a page without the shell at `/w/<code>/core/panel-test/unframed`. The core's application 1.4.0, `maxitor-web` 1.26.0, the installer 1.3.0 and the runner 1.2.0 for their graph files. ([#109](https://github.com/bystrovmaxim/gnomon/issues/109))
- **The frame every plugin lives in.** An organization's screen at `/w/<code>` is the core's frame: the tree on the left, the top row with the organization, the path and the person, and the right panel, a grey field with dots. The tree is the core's own `navigation.yaml` (Settings → Plugins, now at `/w/<code>/core/settings/plugins`; the old address leads there) and every node of the plugins enabled in the organization: a version's nodes are read when it is checked into `plugins.plugin_node`, and installing or enabling a plugin refuses it with `navigation` when its nodes would not hold — attached to a plugin it does not depend on, to a node that is not there, or using a parameter nothing above defines; a version whose list or filter reads no action of its wheel is rejected. `GET /api/v1/organizations/{code}` answers the tree filtered by the person's roles and the plugins in dependency order; a list node's records load through the gateway at the panel's moment, 50 at a time. A plugin's page opens in a transparent frame over the panel and talks to the core by the panel protocol: one history entry per change in the window's address, the page told when the address or "now" moves (`GET /api/v1/organizations/{code}/watermark`, which a plugin's change now moves too). The core draws a page's mode pill, view switch, filters and legend from its `controls`, the same everywhere; administrators have a test page of the panel at `/w/<code>/core/panel-test`. The gateway sends a plugin's page opened by itself to its panel address, lets only the site frame it, and serves the page's own scripts. A plugin runner has a name, `MAXITOR_RUNNER_NAME`, and starts and removes only its own containers and networks, so two stands share a host. ([#74](https://github.com/bystrovmaxim/gnomon/issues/74))
- **Plugins run, behind one site with one sign-in.** A new process, `maxitor-plugin-runner` (`pip install "maxitor[plugin-runner]"`), keeps every plugin version that some organization has enabled running in Docker containers of its own: images built once from the version's wheel, a backend and, when the wheel carries its pages, a frontend, each as an unprivileged user with no capabilities, a read-only file system, limits of memory, CPU and processes, a network of its version's own and its port on `127.0.0.1` only. A plugin reads neither another plugin's password nor anything of the core's settings. A container that exits is started again after a growing pause; a version no organization runs is removed. The core's application is the gateway: a request to `/p/<name>/**` passes only for an active member of the organization of its address, to a plugin enabled there, and reaches the plugin without the core's cookies, with a token the core signed for that plugin alone — `EdDSA`, 60 seconds, the person, the organization and the person's role in the core, `member` or `administrator`. Plugins check it by the core's JWKS, `GET /api/v1/jwks`. A plugin calls a plugin it depends on through the gateway as the same person, and works with no person by the service key its version gets on deployment. New settings: `MAXITOR_GATEWAY_KEY_FILE` for the core, and for the runner `MAXITOR_PLUGIN_GATEWAY_URL`, `MAXITOR_PLUGIN_DB_ADDRESS`, `MAXITOR_PLUGIN_DB_CONTAINER`, `MAXITOR_PLUGIN_FIND_LINKS`, `MAXITOR_PLUGIN_MEMORY`, `MAXITOR_PLUGIN_CPUS`. A plugin declares no roles of its own any more: its passport's `roles` and a node's role other than `member` or `administrator` are refused. ([#75](https://github.com/bystrovmaxim/gnomon/issues/75))
- **Plugins in an organization.** An organization's administrator opens Settings at the foot of its tree → Plugins (`/w/<code>/settings/plugins`): the installed plugins and the marketplace, one search over both with a count on each tab, a plugin's details, installing with its checks, disabling and enabling — each refused, with the plugins in the way named, when it would leave an enabled plugin without what it needs — a failed install retried or dropped, and a private plugin added by a release's Package URL and `sha256` or by uploading its wheel, which no other organization sees. Everyone else gets 404 there. The core's backend answers `GET /api/v1/organizations/{code}/plugins`, `GET /api/v1/organizations/{code}/plugins/{passport_id}` and `POST /api/v1/plugins/{install,retry,drop,disable,enable,private,private/upload}`; `GET /api/v1/organizations/{code}` also answers the enabled plugins with their sections, which the tree shows. ([#82](https://github.com/bystrovmaxim/gnomon/issues/82))
- **The core's domain `plugins`** in a schema of its own: a plugin of the marketplace, its versions with their dependencies and navigation sections, and a plugin in an organization, every table versioned, every line a typed event. ([#82](https://github.com/bystrovmaxim/gnomon/issues/82))
- **`maxitor-plugin-installer`** (`pip install "maxitor[plugin-installer]"`): a process of its own, the only holder of the role `maxitor_installer`'s password. It checks a version — download, `sha256`, passport 2, `schema.yaml`, the core's range — and deploys it once for all of Maxitor in one transaction: the schema `<name>_<x>_<y>_<z>`, the user `plg_<name>_<x>_<y>_<z>` with a SCRAM verifier, the tables with their version tables, the keys to the core, the rights; the plain password only in the version's `plugin.env`. `add-version` and `add-wheel` fill the marketplace. ([#82](https://github.com/bystrovmaxim/gnomon/issues/82))
- **What a plugin needs of the core comes through functions**: `change_time`, `watermark`, `is_member`, `member_organization` and `member_name` of the schema `organization`, executable by the group `maxitor_plugin` only. A version's user sees nothing else: no core table, no other schema, nothing in `public`. ([#82](https://github.com/bystrovmaxim/gnomon/issues/82))

### Changed

- **Maxitor has a repository of its own**, [bystrovmaxim/gnomon](https://github.com/bystrovmaxim/gnomon), with the Open Gnomon Protocol beside it. The package, its commands and its extras are unchanged; the GitHub Action of `graph-builder` is now `bystrovmaxim/gnomon/packages/maxitor/tools/graph-builder@maxitor-v<version>`, and the setup skill installs from this repository.

### Fixed

- **A plugin that cannot be built says why, and the local stand builds it against the checkout's wheels.** The runner builds an image with plain progress and reports the first error of the step that failed — `The images of io.github.bystrovmaxim.notes 0.9.0 could not be built: ERROR: Could not find a version that satisfies the requirement maxitor-plugin-sdk<2,>=1.5 (from versions: 1.4.0)` — where it used to report Docker Desktop's `View build details` link. `scripts/core-stand.sh up` builds the wheels plugins take from `.core-stand/find-links` — AOA's and `maxitor-plugin-sdk` — from the checkout on every start, so a stand folder brought to a newer `main` no longer keeps `maxitor-plugin-sdk` 1.4.0 for a template that needs 1.5; its Python processes write their output unbuffered, so the runner's warnings reach a log file at once. Maxitor 4.4.1. ([#112](https://github.com/bystrovmaxim/gnomon/issues/112))
- **A domain's ERD frames only its own resources.** In the physical view the ERD of one domain drew a frame for every resource of the model — on the AOA demo 29, almost all of them empty — though a resource that keeps none of the domain's tables has nothing to do with it. It now frames only the resources that keep the domain's tables or their 1-hop neighbours, and its Resources filter lists the same; the ERD of all domains still frames every resource, an empty one too. ([#78](https://github.com/bystrovmaxim/gnomon/issues/78))

### Added

- **The ERD has a logical view and a physical one.** A pill at the top of the ERD switches between them, each with what it frames counted beside it, and the address keeps the choice as `model=logical` or `model=physical`. The logical view shows what the business is made of: a frame per domain, its entities and the relations between them, and nothing about databases. The physical view shows where the data lies: a frame per resource of the model — one that keeps nothing too, empty — with a frame inside it for each domain whose entities it keeps. An entity kept in several databases is drawn in each, marked `×2` or more, its copies joined by a dashed line, and hovering one lights them all; a relation is drawn between every pair of copies, dashed where it crosses from one database to another, since only an action can join that data; entities kept nowhere gather under **Not stored**. A table's card names its domain and every resource that keeps it, comparing two versions marks a table whose set of resources changed, and a room shares the view. The data source is gone from every screen with **Boundary**, which the physical view replaces; an old address that still carries them opens as if it did not. A graph older than protocol 1.6 does not say where entities lie, so its Physical half is disabled and says why. Maxitor itself now runs on the AOA where a resource is a database: its graph store lists the entity it builds, and every resource is named by its kind. ([#67](https://github.com/bystrovmaxim/gnomon/issues/67))
- **Protocol 2.0: a resource names its entities.** The graph builder and the comparison read graphs of protocol 2.0, in which a resource — a database — names each entity it keeps by a `resource_entity` edge, and there is no data source; every 1.x version is read as before. Maxitor's store keeps one table of links from a resource to the entities it keeps, filled from a 2.0 graph directly and from the `entity_resource` edges of a 1.6–1.8 graph turned around, so the ERD's resources and contours look the same at any of these versions; where several resources keep one entity, the ERD draws it in the first by id. Maxitor reads no data source any more: a graph of 1.7–1.8 opens as one of 1.6 would, without the Data sources sections, the outer contours and the ERD per data source and per resource, and `list-entities` names no data source. The constitution's data-model rules say the same (version 1.5.0): a resource is a database and names its entities, a relation may join entities of any resources, and the pool is the application's choice.
- **Contours are easier to use.** The control that folds a contour stays 16 pixels on screen at any zoom, takes a click over a wider area and says "Fold" or "Unfold"; on a folded box it stands beside the box, clear of its name. Resting on a resource contour or a folded box opens a card like a table's: the icon the full graph draws — the full graph now draws a data source with an icon and colour of its own —, the name, where it lives, its description and the tables it holds, folded at first. A room shares the hover and the card of a folded box and of a line between folded boxes, and Side by side passes such a line to the other half by its two ends. Moving the diagram no longer makes the fields lag behind their tables.
- **The ERD follows the logical data model.** A graph of protocol 1.8 relates entities of any databases, and every resource that stores an entity names its data source. With Boundary on, a relation line may now leave a data source's contour for another's, so the reader sees which parts of the model live in which store and which relations join two stores — on the AOA demo, the billing warehouse's chargeback ticket points at the shop database's order, and hovering the warehouse's contour lights that line. "No data source" appears only for graphs of 1.6–1.7. Maxitor's own model follows the rules too: its graph rows live in `GraphDataSource`, the DuckDB kept in its process, and its own graph is 1.8. Maxitor builds on AOA `c879d38d`, and the constitution's rules 24, 25 and 26 say the data model is logical (version 1.4.0).
- **The ERD shows where each entity is stored.** A graph of protocol 1.6 says which resource — a database, for one — stores each entity, and AOA keeps every relation inside one resource, so the tables of a resource are the whole data model of that store. The filter panel gains a **Resources** section, shown even for one resource; switching one off hides its tables. **Boundary**, beside 1-hop, draws each resource's tables inside one rounded contour with the resource's name in its header, and a specialization block inside its contour; it starts on. The gear shift paints by resource too. A resource's colour comes from the server and depends on the graph alone, so it is the same on every diagram and for everyone in a room, and a room shares the resources switched off, Boundary and the paint; the address carries them (`off_resources`, `boundary`, `paint=resources`). A table's card reads `Resource: <name>`. In a comparison a table counts as changed by its resource only when both versions name one and they differ, so comparing a 1.5 graph with a 1.6 one marks nothing. `list-entities` gives each entity `resource_id` and `resource_label` and lists the graph's `resources` with their colours. A graph older than 1.6 looks as before. Maxitor's own entity, `NodeCoreEntity`, is stored by `DuckDBGraphResource`, and Maxitor builds on AOA `537c61d4`.
- **Data source contours read, copy, fold, and have a place of their own.** On the ERD with Boundary on, a data source's name is written once, in its contour's header, and clicking a resource's or a data source's name copies it. Every contour folds into one box with a click on the faint minus in its corner: the box names what it holds, a table still drawn keeps a line of its own for each relation to it, and the relations between two folded boxes become one line, wider with their number and plated with it (`×3`); hovering that line lists its relations, clicking it unfolds both ends. The folded contours travel in the address (`folded`) and a room shares them, as it now also shares Boundary, the resources and data sources switched off, and painting by them. The left panel gains **Data sources**, after Domains, for a graph that has one: each data source holds the ERD of the whole data source and one per resource, and **No data source** one per resource that names none; such an ERD draws exactly the tables stored there (`data_source=` / `resource=` in the address).
- **Hovering a resource contour shows what the resource links to.** On the ERD with Boundary on, resting the pointer on a resource contour's border or name lights every line from one of its tables to a table of another resource, keeps the tables at both ends bright, widens the border, and dims everything else — everything, when the resource has no such line. The hover label is the resource's full id, and a room shares the hover, so a follower sees the same lines lit.
- **The ERD shows the databases resources share.** A graph of protocol 1.7 says which resources work with one database — its data source — and AOA keeps every relation inside one data source, or inside one resource that names none. With Boundary on, the resources of one data source are drawn inside one outer dashed contour headed by the data source's name, so a relation between tables of two resources of one database stays inside it. The filter panel gains a **Data sources** section — one row per data source of the diagram and one **No data source** row — shown only when the diagram has one; a Resources row names its data source beside it. The gear shift paints by data source, in a colour the server gives from the graph alone. A table's card reads `Resource: <name> · Data source: <name>`. The data sources switched off are a page setting a room shares and the address carries (`off_data_sources`). `list-entities` gives each resource its `data_source_id` and lists the graph's `data_sources` with their colours. A graph of 1.0–1.6 looks as before. Maxitor builds on AOA `9bd61c82`, and the constitution's resource rules 24, 26 and 53 cover the data source (version 1.3.0).
- **The logical data model**: the graph builder and the comparison read graphs of protocol 1.8, in which a relation may join entities of any data sources and every resource that stores an entity names its data source, so a graph written at 1.8 is checked and compared.
- **Resources that share a store**: the graph builder and the comparison read graphs of protocol 1.7, which adds the node `DataSource` and the edge `resource_data_source` from a resource to it, so a graph written at 1.7 is checked and compared.
- **Where an entity is stored**: the graph builder and the comparison read graphs of protocol 1.6, which adds the edge `entity_resource` from an entity to its resource, so a graph written at 1.6 is checked and opens in Maxitor.
- **A cache stored entity by entity**: Maxitor reads graphs of protocol 1.5 — the `Cache` kind `entity_key` and the edge `@cache_entity_key`. The graph builder and the comparison read 1.5 as well.
- **The cache in the graph**: Maxitor reads graphs of protocol 1.4 — the `Cache` node of each cache declaration, drawn with a glyph and colour of its own, and the edges `@cache_key`, `@cache_read`, `@cache_write`, `@cache_invalidate`, `caches` and `invalidates`. The graph builder and the comparison read 1.4 as well.

### Changed

- **Loading a graph returns its text, not the DuckDB graph**: `LoadAOAServiceAction` downloads, checks and summarizes a service's graph, and its Result carries the graph's JSON text; the server builds the DuckDB graph from that text. A Result holds only values with a JSON form, and AOA's graph build now refuses one that does not. A broken graph still fails the load as before.

### Added

- **`repo-graph-port` follows a moved file**: when the folder of a graph file or a version file moves, the port asks git where the file was and reads each older version there, so the history keeps everything before the move — for a public link too. `graph_file_before` and `version_file_before` remain for a move git cannot see.
- **A history is called by what you opened**: the folder that holds its graph file, or the repository for a graph at its root, instead of the repository for every graph in it; a private history keeps its id. The name heads the version picker, titles the tab and stands in Recent; rest on it to read its full path, click it to copy the path.
- **Maxitor explores events step by step.** The Events pages open on one layer — every domain, every event, or every action that emits, as a pill at the top chooses — and you follow the graph link by link through ports, each showing what it leads to and how many. A domain tells how many events its vocabulary holds (an empty vocabulary is still one), or how many it emits without one; its actions port shows all its actions, so you see what events do not cover yet. A path passes each kind once: the second layer leads on to the kind not yet on the path, and the third joins back to what is already on the screen, so from an event you reach the actions of a domain and see which of them emit it. Filters fit what is on the screen — domains, coverage, the standing of events, kinds of method, actions with or without events — and any of them can paint it. Rest on a line to read its method, on an event to read its fields, producers and domains. A comparison of two versions paints the changes and counts them on closed ports, so none hides behind one; the address keeps what you opened.
- **Maxitor opens a graph's history** served by `repo-graph-port` and shows any version: a version button and a picker with a time axis — its handle dragged to a version in One as in Diff — of merged pull requests, direct pushes, open pull requests by review state and Live; version numbers from tags, release flags, a card per point with who made and merged it, its size and its graph change; the address bar carries the key, so copying it shares the view. `repo-graph-port` adds each version's contribution and graph change, pull requests read from merge commits, and the open pull requests.
- **`repo-graph-port`**, a service that reads a graph's history from its repository: versions with their pull requests, tags and releases, and any version's graph; read keys for private repositories through a GitHub App, links for public ones, GitHub's webhooks, and without them a fetch on read once ten minutes have passed. `pip install "maxitor[repo-graph-port]"`, command `repo-graph-port`.

## [2.3.0] – 2026-10-27
